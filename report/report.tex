\section{Introduction}
% charging law and time constant go here

This document introduces the charging behavior of a resistor-capacitor (RC) network. When a capacitor C is charged through a series resistor R from a constant supply $V_0$, the capacitor voltage rises according to

\begin{equation}
    V(t) = V_0(1-e^{-t/RC}),
\end{equation}

\noindent
where the product RC has units of seconds and is called the time constant, $\tau=RC$
